From 2018 through 2025, the week-1 AP Top 25 No. 1 made the College Football Playoff 5 times in 8. The week-1 top 4 made it 17 times in 32.

That is the preseason ballot as a playoff ticket. It was never a lock. When the stored field went from four teams to 12, the ticket got cheaper in a specific way: more of the field came from 11 through 25, and from teams who were not in that week-1 poll at all.

The week-1 No. 1 is not the field

Five of eight week-1 No. 1s got in. The three who did not: Alabama in 2022, Georgia in 2023, Texas in 2025. Alabama had made it as week-1 No. 1 in 2018 and 2021. Clemson made it in 2019 and 2020. Georgia made it in 2024.

Texas in 2025 is the 12-team version of the miss. Week-1 No. 1. Not in the field. The stored format that year is twelve_team_2025.

Same 24 slots, different week-1 shape

From 2018 through 2023 the CFP stored four teams per season, 24 slots. Twelve of those 24 were week-1 top 4. Two were 11 through 25. Two were not in that week-1 AP Top 25.

In 2024 and 2025 the stored field was 12 teams per season, also 24 slots. Five of 24 were week-1 top 4. Seven were 11 through 25. Six were not in that week-1 poll. Combine 11-25 with unranked and you get 13 of 24 slots the week-1 top 10 did not occupy.

2024 went 4-for-4. 2025 went 1-for-4.

In 2024, twelve_team_2024, all four week-1 top 4 made the field. That did not make the ballot the field. Four CFP teams that season were not in the week-1 AP Top 25: Boise State (seed 3), Arizona State (seed 4), Indiana (seed 10), SMU (seed 11).

In 2025, twelve_team_2025, only one of the week-1 top 4 made it. Texas, the week-1 No. 1, did not. Two CFP teams were not in that week-1 poll: Tulane (seed 11) and James Madison (seed 12).

A 12-team field did not freeze the week-1 top 4

2024 put all four through. 2025 put one through. Same stored team_count. Opposite week-1 conversion.

The CFP teams who were not in week 1

Unranked here means absent from that season's week-1 AP Top 25, not a claim about later polls. Eight CFP teams in 2018–2025 fit. Two of them came in four_team seasons: Michigan in 2021, seed 2, and TCU in 2022, seed 3.

The other six came in 2024 and 2025. The names stay on those CFP seasons.
