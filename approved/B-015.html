The SEC’s talent pile is thicker than its AP pile

Fourteen of the 25 highest 2025 talent teams are SEC. Nine of the 25 AP slots are. That is the widest gap in this poll, and it is not a score. It is two different 25-seat boards.

The 2025 talent top 10 is even tighter: SEC 5, Big Ten 3, ACC 1, Independents 1, Big 12 0. Half of last year’s top 10 talent list is SEC. The 2026 AP is not.

The Big 12’s AP pile has no talent-top25 underneath it

Four 2026 AP slots. Zero teams in the 2025 talent top 25. Zero in the talent top 10. Sixteen Big 12 teams are in the 134. They hold 12.8% of all 2025 talent points. None of that stock cracked the talent top 25. The ballot still found four seats.

The Big Ten is the milder version of the same direction: 8 AP slots, 6 of the 2025 talent top 25. The ACC is the other way, slightly: 3 AP slots, 4 of the talent top 25.

Independents stay Independents

Notre Dame is the Independent seat on both 25-team boards, and the Independent seat in the 2025 talent top 10. FBS Independents are 2 of 134 on the full 2025 talent list. That 1-of-10 is not a conference folded into a power league. It is stored as Independent, and it stays that way here.

The No. 14 tie still counts as two AP slots. USC is Big Ten. BYU is Big 12. If you treat 14 as one rank, the slot math breaks. The poll issued two seats.

Two 14s, two conferences, two slots

USC and BYU are both ranked 14 in the 2026 AP. That is a Big Ten slot and a Big 12 slot. The Big Ten’s 8 and the Big 12’s 4 already include them.

Zero AP slots is not zero talent teams

Sun Belt 14, American Athletic 13, Mid-American 12, Conference USA 10, Pac-12 8, Mountain West 8. Those are 2025 talent-all counts among 134. None of those conferences have a 2026 AP slot. None have a talent-top25 team either. They are still on the talent list.

The SEC and the Big Ten each hold 15.8% of all 2025 talent points. The SEC turned that stock into 14 of 25 talent-top25 seats. The Big Ten turned the same share of points into 6. That is concentration inside the top of the list, not a conference trophy.

Top talent is more concentrated than AP slots. The ballot is more spread.

Fourteen of 25 talent-top25 seats in one conference. Four AP seats in a conference with zero talent-top25 teams. The 2026 AP is not a map of the 2025 talent top 25 by league.
