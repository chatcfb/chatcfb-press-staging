Houston, cheapest returning seat in the poll

A tie at 14, two different machines

The AP tied USC and BYU at 14. Both 839 points. That tie is the ballot.

BYU is 2nd among the 25 in 2026 returning production, 78.5%, 15th of 136. On 2025 talent, BYU is 24th among these 25 and 70th of 134, at 649.36, a tick above Indiana. High returning share, low 2025 talent: the AP split the difference and landed on 14.

USC is 11th among the 25 in returning production, 59.2%, and 14th among these 25 in 2025 talent. Same AP number as BYU, different lists underneath it. One 14 is a production team the 2025 composite did not love. The other 14 is sitting on its 2025 talent rank inside this poll.

What the ballot left on the floor

That is a different piece. The short version sits here because it is the same pricing question, pointed outside the 25.

Off the 2026 AP, on the other lists.

Clemson is 7th of 134 in 2025 talent, 918.43, and not in the 2026 Top 25. LSU is 6th of 134 on that list and 11th in the AP. Western Michigan is 1st of 136 in 2026 returning production, 99.7%, and also not in the poll. Houston, first inside the 25, is 11th of 136.

The 2026 AP Top 25 is a market in the only sense that matters here: it assigned prices that do not match last year's talent list or this year's returning offense, and it did that most loudly with Indiana, Alabama, Houston, and BYU.

Read the board as the ballot. Do not read it as a production ranking, and do not read it as 2025 talent.
