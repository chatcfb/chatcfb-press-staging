The 2026 AP Top 25 is not one kind of program. Three ranked teams have a coach who has been in the chair since 2018 or earlier, and a 2026 returning offense that still sits inside the top eight of this poll.

One has the longest job in the field and almost none of last year's offense back. One hired a new coach and kept the production. Two hired new coaches and did not.

The long jobs with an offense still attached

BYU is the clean pile. Kalani Sitake, year 11, started in 2016, 84-45 in 129 games. 78.5% of last year's offensive PPA is back, 2nd among these 25, 15th of 136 teams with 2026 returning-production data. The AP tied BYU at 14. The 2025 talent composite had BYU 24th among these 25 and 70th of 134. Continuity here is the coach and the offense, not last year's talent list.

Georgia is Kirby Smart in year 11, 117-21 in 138 games, 68.7% back, 7th among the 25. 2025 talent: 1st of 134. Ohio State is Ryan Day in year 9, 82-12 in 94 games, 68.3% back, 8th among the 25. 2025 talent: 3rd of 134. The top of the poll looks like a place. BYU is the same stack without the talent layer.

The long job without the roster

Iowa is Kirk Ferentz, started in 1999, 28 seasons, 213-128 in 341 games. That is the longest active tenure in this Top 25. 34.2% of last year's offensive PPA is back, 21st among the 25, 83rd of 136. 2025 talent: 42nd of 134, 20th among these 25.

The office did not turn over. The offense did. A long job is not the same thing as a returning offense. Iowa is the only team in this poll that sits in both of those chairs at once.

Ferentz has the oldest job on the 2026 AP board. Iowa also has one of the thinnest returning offenses in this poll. Continuity stopped at the sideline.

New coach, production still there

Michigan hired Kyle Whittingham on December 27, 2025. Zero games in Ann Arbor. 69.2% of offensive PPA is back, 6th among the 25, 22nd of 136. 2025 talent: 11th of 134, 10th among these 25. The AP put that 16th.

He went 177-88 at Utah from 2004 through 2025. New head coach. Not a stripped roster. The reset is the office. The 2026 returning offense did not reset with it.

New coach, production gone

LSU hired Lane Kiffin on November 30, 2025. Zero games. 21.2% back, 23rd among the 25, 97th of 136. He went 55-19 at Ole Miss from 2020 through 2025. The 2025 talent composite still has LSU 6th of 134. The 2026 AP has LSU 11th. The returning offense does not match either number.

Penn State hired Matt Campbell on December 8, 2025. Zero games. 7.6% back, last among the 25, 123rd of 136. He went 72-55 at Iowa State from 2016 through 2025. 2025 talent: 10th of 134, 9th among these 25. Last in this poll in returning production. Still 18th in the AP.

A new coach is not a geology. Michigan hired one and kept the offense. LSU and Penn State hired one and did not. Iowa never hired one, and the offense left anyway.

Read the board as three layers that do not have to move together: the job, the 2026 returning offense, and the 2025 talent list. In this poll, they mostly do not.
